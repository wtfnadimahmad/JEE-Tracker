Frequently asked questions

Is the JEE Study Tracker free?

Yes. The tracker is completely free, needs no sign-up and has no ads.

Where is my study data stored?

All data is stored locally in your browser. Nothing is uploaded to a server. You can export a JSON backup from Settings and import it on another device.

Does it cover the full JEE Main and Advanced syllabus?

It comes with 74 chapters built in: 23 Physics, 27 Chemistry (Physical, Organic and Inorganic) and 24 Mathematics chapters, each with a 7-stage checklist from theory to testing.

How does mock test analysis work?

Log each JEE Main or JEE Advanced mock with subject-wise marks, correct, incorrect and unattempted questions, time taken and the chapters it covered. The tracker charts your score trend and subject-wise performance.

How does the tracker find my weak chapters?

Every question practice entry records attempted, correct and incorrect questions per chapter. Chapters with low accuracy are flagged as weak on your dashboard so you know what to revise.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. It works in any modern mobile or desktop browser and can be added to your home screen like an app.
